# Wikimedia Commons library (original notes)


All tracks: Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 3.0 or 4.0. Commercial use including monetized videos allowed. **Credit required** in caption/description. Files are converted to high-quality MP3. `_preview.mp3` = 13 s strongest section at -14 LUFS. `_REEL_<mood>.mp3` = previews with spoken track names.

Credit template: `Music: "<Title>" by <Artist> (<licence>) via Wikimedia Commons`

BPM was not measured. Energy is based on integrated loudness (LUFS, louder = denser) and loudness range (LRA).

## Caveats
- Pixabay Music and Uppbeat block automated downloads from this box (HTTP 403/429), so none are included. Uppbeat's free tier needs an account and credit; check its terms before use.
- Infraction and Alexi Action publish as "No Copyright Music" on YouTube. Some of their tracks may be in Content ID via distributors; if a claim appears, dispute it with the CC BY licence and the Commons page. Not verified per track.
- CC BY 3.0/4.0 needs attribution: title, artist, licence and source link.
